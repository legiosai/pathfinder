About

What it is

pathfinder helps you pick an IT certification and plan the study. It is open source and runs entirely in your browser: your choices and progress stay on this device.

Where the data comes from

Prices change and vary by country. Confirm with the issuer before you pay.

Levels

Levels use trail difficulty signs:

    “For me” score

    Up to 100 points: 45 for the skills you want to learn, 20 for your role, 20 for the skills you already use and 15 for how the level fits your experience. It sorts the list. It does not predict a pass.
