About pathfinder

pathfinder is an open-source tool for choosing and preparing IT certifications. It is a static page: your profile, shortlist and plan progress stay in your browser's local storage and are never sent anywhere.

How the match score works

Each certification gets up to 100 points: 45 for overlap with the skills you want to grow, 20 for your role, 20 for skills you already use, and 15 for how its level fits your experience. A different assessment style than the one you prefer costs 8 points. Over-budget and restricted credentials are listed separately. The score is a sorting aid. It is not a pass probability or a career prediction.

How study hours are estimated

The range for each level moves toward its low end the more of the certification's skills you already use, and it grows by a quarter if you are new to the field. Treat it as a first guess. A diagnostic in your first session tells you more.

Where the data comes from

Provider facts (fees, formats, domains) come from issuer pages, each linked from the certification's details with the date it was checked. Candidate reports are self-reported experiences. They show how others prepared and do not set a pass rate. Prices change and vary by country, so confirm everything with the issuer before you pay or book.

Certification names and codes belong to their issuers. pathfinder is not affiliated with any of them.

Contribute
