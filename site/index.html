    About

    pathfinder helps you pick an IT certification and plan the study. It is open source and runs entirely in your browser: your choices and progress are stored on this device only.

    Data

    Prices, formats and exam domains come from the issuers' own pages, linked on each certification with the date they were checked. Prices change and vary by country, so confirm with the issuer before you pay.

    “For me” ranking

    Up to 100 points: 45 for the skills you want to learn, 20 for your role, 20 for the skills you already use and 15 for how the level fits your experience. It sorts the list. It does not predict a pass.
